Made to order

Your colours, your size, your story

Love a piece but want it in a different colour? Need a cardigan in a particular size, or a painting for a particular wall? Ritu is happy to make it for you.

  • Tell Ritu what you have in mind — colours, size, a photo for inspiration
  • She'll suggest options, a price and a realistic timeline
  • You pay to confirm, and she gets started
  • Pick up in Auckland or have it couriered within New Zealand

Before you order

Delivery, care and gifting

Pick up or courier

Collect for free in Auckland, or choose courier delivery anywhere in New Zealand — postage is quoted when Ritu confirms your order.

Caring for knits

Hand-wash cool, press out the water in a towel and dry flat. Each piece comes with a little care card.

Wrapped for giving

Add a gift message in your order note and your piece will be wrapped and ready to hand over.

Can't find quite the right piece?

Tell Ritu what you're looking for. If it can be knitted, stitched or painted, she'd love to try.
