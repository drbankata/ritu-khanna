How it works

Four easy steps

  1. Choose

    Add dishes or a party menu below and pick your pickup date — at least 2 days ahead.

  2. Send

    Send your order to Ritu. She replies to confirm the date and final total.

  3. Pay in advance

    Your order is confirmed once payment is received. Then the cooking begins.

  4. Pick up

    Collect your food fresh and hot or ready to reheat, packed in sturdy disposable containers.

    Good to know

    Before you order

    Order ahead

    Please order at least 2 days before pickup. Festival weeks fill up quickly — book early for Diwali, Christmas and long weekends.

    Pay to confirm

    Ritu confirms availability and the final total, then your order is locked in once payment is received. Changes are welcome until 48 hours before pickup.

    Packed to go

    Food is packed in sturdy, disposable containers with lids — easy to carry, serve and clear away. Reheating notes are included.

    Pickup in Auckland

    The pickup address and a time window are shared when your order is confirmed.

    Vegetarian friendly

    Plenty of vegetarian dishes on every menu, and many dishes can be adapted. Just ask.

    Allergies

    Food is cooked in a home kitchen that also handles nuts, dairy, gluten, eggs and sesame. Please tell Ritu about any allergy when you order.

    Planning something bigger?

    Weddings, office events or a menu you can't find here — tell Ritu about your occasion and she'll put together a menu and quote.
